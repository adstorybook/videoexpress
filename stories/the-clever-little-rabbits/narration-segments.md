## Segment 01 — 254 characters

At the edge of a green wood, three little rabbits huddled behind a round red door. A dark, shaggy tail slid underneath. It was not their mother's bright white tail. The kits did not touch the latch. How could they keep safe? Let's rewind to that morning.

## Segment 02 — 298 characters

The three little ones were curious, quick, and clever. They loved to hop through the garden, but they always listened when their mother gave them an important rule. One morning, Mother Rabbit needed to visit the meadow for fresh carrots and herbs. She packed a basket and called her children close.

## Segment 03 — 234 characters

“I will be back before the afternoon light turns gold,” she said. “Until then, stay together, keep the door shut, and do not open it for anyone. If you are unsure, look through the little window and wait for me.” The rabbits promised.

## Segment 04 — 317 characters

They practiced their plan: one would watch, one would listen, and one would remind them all to stay calm. Mother Rabbit waved and set off along the woodland path. The kits watched her white tail disappear behind the trees. They played a quiet counting game, tidied their toys, and listened to the wind brush the roof.

## Segment 05 — 310 characters

Soon, a big gray wolf in a floppy brown hat came wandering down the path. He heard the rabbits inside and tapped on the red door. “Little rabbits, open up,” he called, trying to make his voice sound soft and familiar. “It is your mother.” The three rabbits looked at one another. They remembered their promise.

## Segment 06 — 289 characters

“Our mother is gentle,” they whispered, “but we must be sure.” So they stayed safely inside and asked the visitor to show them something only their mother could have: her bright white tail. The wolf hesitated. Then he bent and pushed his own tail through the narrow crack beneath the door.

## Segment 07 — 265 characters

The rabbits peered down, thinking hard about what to do next. The rabbits knew at once that the tail outside the door was dark and shaggy, not the bright white tail they expected. They did not open the door. Instead, they whispered together and made a careful plan.

## Segment 08 — 274 characters

When the wolf pushed his tail farther through the crack, all three kits leaned their weight against the door and shut it firmly. The wolf’s tail was caught outside, while the rabbits remained safely together indoors. The wolf gave a surprised yelp and tugged. The door held.

## Segment 09 — 269 characters

He pulled again, then tried turning this way and that, but the clever rabbits kept it shut. Their game had become a little noisy, so Mother Rabbit, returning with her basket of carrots, hurried up the path. She saw the wolf at the door and understood what had happened.

## Segment 10 — 309 characters

Mother Rabbit stood bravely between the wolf and her home. She raised the small walking stick she carried on the trail and gave the wolf a light, comic tap on the top of his hat. Bonk! The wolf jumped so high that his hat wobbled. In the confusion, he pulled hard, slipped free, and hurried off down the path.

## Segment 11 — 193 characters

A tiny tuft of fur came loose from the end of his tail and floated gently onto the grass. The wolf was startled, but he was not badly hurt. He ran far away and did not bother the rabbits again.

## Segment 12 — 298 characters

Mother Rabbit checked each of her children, hugged them close, and praised them for staying together, remembering the rule, and thinking carefully. The rabbits helped carry the carrots into the cottage. That evening they shared warm soup by the window while the forest settled into a peaceful hush.

## Segment 13 — 287 characters

They learned that courage does not mean opening the door to danger. Courage can mean pausing, asking questions, and working together until help arrives. And from then on, whenever a visitor knocked, the three little rabbits remembered: listen carefully, stay safe, and trust a good plan.
