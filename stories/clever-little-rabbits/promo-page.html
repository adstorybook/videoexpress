A tiny mystery at the door

Mother Rabbit steps out for carrots, leaving a simple promise: stay together and keep the door closed. When a visitor arrives, the kits ask one careful question before they decide what to do.

Talk about it together

What helped the rabbits make a safe choice? This story gives families a gentle way to talk about listening, pausing, and asking a trusted grown-up for help.
