# Five-Story Video Express Handler

This handler is for producing a series of five separate family stories, each targeting approximately ten minutes.

## Non-negotiable generation settings

- Keep **Automatically enhance my image prompt** unchecked.
- Keep the user's image prompt unchanged.
- Use 16:9 Landscape and Full HD for YouTube.
- Use Creative mode only when it does not change the approved characters or plot.
- Use Consistent Character with a fixed reference image for each story.
- Do not reuse a clip from another story.
- Delete clearly incorrect generated assets before timeline assembly.

## Story production loop

For each story:

1. Choose one moral and write the complete plot before generating.
2. Break the plot into connected parts: hook, setup, problem, escalation, turning point, solution, lesson, and epilogue.
3. Use the same character reference and exact character description in every part.
4. Generate parts with short natural dialogue and explicit scene continuity.
5. Inspect each generated asset before adding it to the timeline.
6. Delete assets with wrong characters, unrelated people, wrong setting, or broken storyline.
7. Add only approved parts to the timeline in story order.
8. Preview the entire story and verify that the timeline does not contain repeats or unrelated footage.
9. Export High / FullHD / MP4.
10. Verify duration and resolution locally.
11. Upload to YouTube with a truthful title and description.
12. Choose the YouTube audience setting accurately; do not change it to manipulate recommendations.
13. Keep the older upload until the new upload is verified.

## Five-story slate

1. **The Shortcut That Wasn't** — rushing and honesty.
2. **The Garden That Needed Both** — cooperation and planning.
3. **The Echo in the Canyon** — checking facts before spreading a claim.
4. **The Promise Clock** — choosing commitments and finishing what matters.
5. **The Lantern on the Hill** — courage, humility, and asking for help.

## Quality gate

Do not upload a story if:

- the plot is out of order;
- characters change identity or clothing without a story reason;
- a scene repeats as filler;
- the moral is missing or unclear;
- the export is not approximately ten minutes;
- the video contains an unrelated person, object, or location.

A retention rate above 90% cannot be guaranteed. Improve retention through a fast opening hook, clear stakes, frequent purposeful visual changes, concise dialogue, emotional payoff, and a satisfying final lesson. Use YouTube retention analytics after publication to revise future stories.
