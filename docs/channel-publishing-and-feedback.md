# TheStorybook_ad publishing, quality, and learning system

Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@TheStorybook_ad

## Quality promise and retention goal

Target **80%+ audience retention** as an aspirational performance goal, not a guarantee. Retention varies by topic, video length, audience, traffic source, and viewer expectations. Review analytics after publishing and use actual channel/video data to refine the next production.

## Production memory: project defaults and correction log

For each finalized video, keep a versioned project manifest and record the user's approved corrections. Reuse these rules on future projects unless the user explicitly changes them.

Record corrections in these categories:
- **Narration/voice:** chosen voice, accent, pronunciation guide, pacing, emotion, pauses, loudness, mix, words to avoid, corrections to specific lines.
- **Visuals:** character/subject continuity, appearance, style, framing, lighting, motion, scene details, prompt edits, and rejected outputs.
- **Editing:** scene order, duration, pacing, transitions, music, sound effects, captions, title cards, and timing adjustments.
- **Brand/channel:** recurring style, audience, format, intro/outro, thumbnail approach, preferred title and description tone.
- **Review outcomes:** version, issue, correction, approved final, and reusable rule.

Suggested correction record:
| Date/version | Timecode or scene | Issue | Requested correction | Applied change | Approved? | Reuse as rule? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Never treat an unapproved draft as a permanent preference. Ask/confirm when a correction appears project-specific or contradicts a previous approved rule.

## Retention-minded story and edit review

Before rendering, check:
- Opening immediately delivers a compelling question, conflict, surprise, or promise; avoid long greetings/intros.
- Title and thumbnail accurately promise what the video delivers.
- Every scene advances the story, reveals something, creates emotion, or resets curiosity.
- Remove repeated information, slow sections, unnecessary establishing shots, dead air, and awkward pauses.
- Use varied visual composition and purposeful movement; avoid distracting or random cuts.
- Build curiosity loops, escalating stakes, and satisfying payoffs; don't use misleading clickbait.
- Voice remains intelligible, natural, consistent, correctly pronounced, and well-paced.
- Music/SFX support mood without masking narration or overpowering.
- Captions are accurate, readable, correctly timed, and safe within the frame.
- Ending pays off the promise and has a concise next action, without dragging.

## Final technical quality gate

Check the export for:
- Correct aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, codec/container, and platform compatibility.
- No black frames, missing media, visual glitches, unintended cuts, flicker, artifacts, or continuity errors.
- Audio clipping, loudness, noise, voice/music balance, sync, and fades.
- Caption spelling, timing, punctuation, and placement.
- Correct final duration, thumbnail, title, description, tags/keywords if appropriate, chapters, and end screen/card plan.
- Copyright/licensing and rights for every asset, music, voice, and source.
- Watch the complete export—not only a sample—before approval.

## Publishing workflow

1. Prepare final video, thumbnail, title, description, captions, and metadata.
2. Review the full rendered video and check the manifest/rights.
3. Present a final review checklist and preview to the user.
4. **Obtain explicit approval for upload and for visibility/scheduling.** Do not publish automatically without this approval.
5. Upload via a connected YouTube publishing integration if available; otherwise provide the prepared assets and exact steps. Confirm the upload's resulting status/link; never claim upload success without verification.
6. After publication, collect performance metrics at useful checkpoints (e.g. 24 hours, 7 days, 28 days), including average view duration, audience retention curve, CTR, impressions, watch time, traffic sources, and subscriber conversion where available.
7. Identify audience drop-off points and wins; turn confirmed insights into future production rules and correction entries.

## YouTube upload readiness checklist

- [ ] Final export watched end-to-end and approved
- [ ] Thumbnail reviewed at mobile size
- [ ] Title/thumbnail promise is honest and compelling
- [ ] Description and captions checked
- [ ] Rights/licensing verified
- [ ] Metadata and audience settings checked
- [ ] Visibility (private/unlisted/public) or schedule time confirmed by user
- [ ] User explicitly approves upload
- [ ] Upload result/link verified
