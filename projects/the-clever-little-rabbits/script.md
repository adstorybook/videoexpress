## Narration and scene plan

### Scene 1 — A cozy rabbit home (0:00–0:30 | 30s)
In a bright little cottage beside a flower-filled meadow lived Mother Rabbit and her three little white rabbits. The three siblings were sweet, clever, and very good. They loved playing together, sharing carrots, and listening to the birds sing outside their window. Their home was warm and cozy, with a little wooden door that kept them safe.

### Scene 2 — Mother Rabbit goes out (0:30–1:00 | 30s)
One sunny morning, Mother Rabbit needed to go out for a little while. She gathered her three little ones close and reminded them to stay inside. “My little rabbits, please keep the door closed. Do not open it to anyone while I am away. Wait until I come home.” The rabbits nodded. “We will, Mother! We promise!” Mother Rabbit smiled, gave them a loving hug, and went down the garden path.

### Scene 3 — A hungry wolf arrives (1:00–1:30 | 30s)
After a while, a big bad wolf came wandering through the meadow. His tummy rumbled. Then he noticed the rabbit cottage. He peeked at the little door and grinned. “Aha! There must be three little rabbits inside!” He hurried up to the door and knocked. BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! The rabbits froze. They knew their mother was still away.

### Scene 4 — The wolf tries to fool them (1:30–2:05 | 35s)
The wolf tried to sound sweet. He copied Mother Rabbit’s voice as best he could. “Little rabbits, it’s your mother! Open the door!” But the rabbits listened carefully. “That is not our mother,” whispered one. “Her voice is much softer,” said another. “And the wolf’s voice is rough and growly!” said the third. They stayed together and kept the door firmly shut.

### Scene 5 — The clever plan (2:05–2:40 | 35s)
The rabbits thought for a moment. Then one had a clever idea. “We know who you are, Mr. Wolf!” they called. “If you really are our mother, put your tail through the crack beneath the door so we can see it!” The wolf, who was not very clever, thought they had believed him. “Of course!” he said, and pushed his tail toward the crack.

### Scene 6 — Bang! The tail is caught (2:40–3:15 | 35s)
The three rabbits quietly got ready. They waited until the wolf's tail was safely through the crack. “Now—together!” they cried. They pushed with all their might. BANG! The door slammed shut and caught the wolf’s tail. “OWWW!” yelped the wolf. He jumped, twisted, and tugged, but the door would not open. The rabbits held tight and stayed safely inside.

### Scene 7 — Mother Rabbit returns (3:15–3:45 | 30s)
Just then, Mother Rabbit came home. She heard the strange noise and saw the wolf trapped outside her door. “What are you doing near my little ones?” she cried. She picked up a stick and struck the wolf on the head, just as the story tells. The wolf yelped and struggled even harder.

### Scene 8 — The wolf runs away (3:45–4:15 | 30s)
With one hard pull, the wolf broke free—but the end of his tail was left behind. He ran away as fast as his legs could carry him, howling all the way across the meadow. The rabbits peeked out carefully. The danger was gone! Mother Rabbit opened her arms and gathered all three little rabbits into a big, safe hug.

### Scene 9 — Safe together (4:15–4:45 | 30s)
“My brave, clever little ones!” Mother Rabbit said. “You listened, stayed together, and did not open the door to a stranger.” The rabbits hugged their mother. “We missed you!” they said. Mother Rabbit checked that they were all safe, then closed the cottage door. Outside, the flowers swayed and the birds sang again.

### Scene 10 — A happy ending (4:45–5:00 | 15s)
And from that day on, the three clever little rabbits remembered: stay together, be careful, and never open the door to someone you do not know. They lived happily with their loving mother. The end.
