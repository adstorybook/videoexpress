## Narration and scene plan

### Scene 1 — Two families in one tree (0:00–0:35 | 35s)
High in a big, leafy tree lived two magpie families. Each mother had two little chicks. Their nests were close together, tucked among the branches. The mothers loved their babies very much. Every morning, they brought food, kept the little chicks warm, and listened to their cheerful chirps. The tree was full of fluttering wings, rustling leaves, and happy little voices.

### Scene 2 — One mother goes to find food (0:35–1:05 | 30s)
One day, one mother magpie looked out from her nest. Her two chicks were hungry, so she promised to return soon with something delicious. “Stay in the nest, my little ones,” she chirped. Then she spread her wings and flew away, searching the forest for food. Her chicks watched her go, waiting patiently.

### Scene 3 — The eagle (1:05–1:40 | 35s)
As Mother Magpie flew through the sky, a large eagle swooped down. Before she could escape, the eagle carried her away. The two little chicks were left alone in their nest. They looked around for their mother, but she was nowhere to be seen. The nest suddenly felt very quiet, and the little chicks became frightened.

### Scene 4 — The chicks cry for help (1:40–2:10 | 30s)
“Mother! Mother!” cried the two chicks. They were hungry and scared. They called again and again, their tiny voices trembling in the branches. In the neighboring nest, the other mother magpie heard them. She stopped what she was doing and listened carefully. Those little cries needed help.

### Scene 5 — A kind neighbor responds (2:10–2:45 | 35s)
The kind mother magpie flew quickly to the other nest. “Oh, dear little ones,” she said gently. She found the two chicks huddled together, frightened and hungry. She softly stroked their heads and comforted them. “Don't cry, children. I am here. You are not alone.” The chicks began to feel a little safer.

### Scene 6 — A promise of care (2:45–3:20 | 35s)
The mother magpie looked at the little chicks with loving eyes. “From now on, I will be your mother, too. Come home with me.” She carefully helped them leave their nest and guided them to her own, close by in the same big tree. She made sure each little chick was safe before settling them beside her.

### Scene 7 — Welcomed into the family (3:20–3:55 | 35s)
At the new nest, her own two chicks looked at the newcomers. Their mother explained that the little ones needed kindness and care. “They will be our family now,” she said. Her chicks moved closer and made room. Soon, all four little chicks were nestled together. They shared warmth, comfort, and the safe feeling of belonging.

### Scene 8 — A loving home (3:55–4:30 | 35s)
The mother magpie cared for all four chicks. She brought them food, tucked them close, and listened as their little voices grew cheerful again. The two rescued chicks no longer felt lonely. Their new siblings played gently, shared the nest, and looked after one another. The tree was filled with happy chirps once more.

### Scene 9 — Kindness makes a family (4:30–5:00 | 30s)
And so, the kind mother magpie and her four little chicks lived happily together. Their nest became a home filled with love. The two families had become one—not because they were all born together, but because they chose to care for one another. When someone needs help, a little kindness can make a very big difference.
