Preserve the same characters, tree, and nest geography. No on-screen text, captions, logos, watermark. Eagle sequence is brief, non-graphic, and emotionally gentle.

## Character and location lock
- Mother A (whose chicks are left behind): adult magpie, glossy black/navy head and wings, white shoulder patches, soft purple-blue sheen; gentle expressive eyes.
- Mother B (neighbor/adopter): adult magpie with matching species design but slightly lighter blue-violet sheen and distinct small crest/eye shape; kind face.
- Chick A1 and A2: the abandoned pair; tiny black-and-white magpie chicks with purple sheen; distinguish with A1 small blue tuft and A2 rounder face.
- Chick B1 and B2: neighbor's own pair; colorful accents—one yellow chest tint, one pink-purple chest tint—distinct shapes, never change markings.
- Eagle: large blue-feathered eagle with orange-yellow beak, strong but not frightening; carry Mother A offscreen or distant silhouette, no attack detail.
- Tree: one large old leafy tree. Nest A is on left branch, Nest B slightly higher/right; keep the same spatial relationship in every shot.

## Scene prompts (visual clips; narration is in script.md)
1. **Two families (35s):** Establish wide of the big tree, two adjacent nests on different branches; both mothers with exactly two chicks each; gentle morning light, birdsong, leaf movement; count all four chicks clearly.
2. **Mother A leaves (30s):** Mother A at left nest, lovingly tells her two chicks to stay, then flies out searching for food; camera follows her briefly and returns to the empty nest with exactly two chicks waiting.
3. **Eagle (35s):** Sky view: blue eagle glides in and gently but decisively carries Mother A away in a distant, non-graphic silhouette; cut to empty sky and nest A, no struggle or violence detail, Mother A does not return in this story.
4. **Crying chicks (30s):** Close/medium of the two chicks huddled in nest A, calling for their mother; wet-eyed but not overly distressed; nest B visible nearby; mother B hears and turns.
5. **Neighbor responds (35s):** Mother B flies from nest B to nest A, lands gently, bends toward the two frightened chicks, softly strokes their heads with wing tip; comforting expression.
6. **Promise (35s):** Mother B reassures the two chicks, then carefully guides them from nest A toward nest B along nearby branch; show the same tree and short distance, no dangerous falls.
7. **Welcome (35s):** At nest B, Mother B explains; her two chicks shift to make room; rescued pair settle beside them; show exactly four chicks in nest B and mother B, warmth and acceptance.
8. **Loving home (35s):** Mother B feeds and shelters all four chicks; four distinct chicks share food and play gently, their colors/markings consistent; cheerful nest, breeze and leaves.
9. **Kindness ending (30s):** Final wide and close-ups: Mother B and all four chicks together, peaceful nest in golden light; camera slowly pulls back through tree leaves for moral about kindness and chosen family. No return or rescue of Mother A.
